# Content audit

Reviewed 13 September 2026. The [original prototype](../prototype/stoic-prayers.html) is preserved unchanged. The published [passage data](../app/content/prayers.json) contains 22 passages and 40 historical translation selections. The remaining 125 prototype selections are preserved in [withheld-transcriptions.json](withheld-transcriptions.json).

The review compared all 165 supplied historical selections with their cited sources and selected alternative transcriptions. Normalization removed differences in case, whitespace, punctuation, and diacritics, and normalized long-s. An exact normalized match is evidence about wording; it is not independently sufficient evidence about authorship, edition date, or the correctness of the source transcription. OCR similarity scores in the withheld file are diagnostic, not confidence ratings. A failed match is not proof that the candidate is wrong.

The publication set uses traceable transcriptions with identifiable translator attribution. Two selections required explicit editorial handling: Higginson’s Discourses 3.5 selection was limited to sections 7–10, and Basore’s Demetrius passage was transcribed with its narrator’s interjection. The linked Perseus Higginson text has the evident OCR error “snare” for “share”; the reader uses “share.” Source retrieval URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [evidence/sources.json](evidence/sources.json). The audit is not a fresh examination of every scanned printed page.

The modern literal English remains an AI-assisted editorial draft, not an independently reviewed scholarly translation. The public editorial-method page states this explicitly. Cleanthes’s disputed line 4 is annotated. No exhaustive “all surviving prayers” or “all public-domain editions” claim remains in the reader.

To add a withheld edition, identify its actual printed or digital witness, verify translator and date, collate the selected passage, record corrections and precise locations, then add it to the published data. Preserve the ancient passage boundaries or disclose additional translation context. Do not promote a candidate on fuzzy OCR similarity alone.
